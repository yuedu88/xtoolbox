Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026 · Last updated: October 12, 2026

Better Everyday (“we,” “our,” or “us”) provides apps for iPhone and iPad, including EPUB2TXT, World Macro, and Currency Converter, as well as this website. We respect your privacy. This policy describes what information these apps and this website do and do not handle. By using them, you acknowledge this policy. If you do not agree, please stop using the applicable app or website.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect or store personal information through our apps. You do not need to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, or other personal details to use their core features. We do not use the apps to build user profiles, and we do not sell or share users’ personal information.

In particular, our apps are not designed to collect:

  • Personal or contact details, such as your name, email address, or phone number;
  • Device identifiers or device details for user profiling;
  • Precise location information;
  • Usage histories for advertising or analytics; or
  • The contents of files you select, except for local processing described below.

2. Files and Access Permissions

EPUB2TXT lets you choose EPUB or TXT files using the file access controls provided by your device. Access is limited to files you select or locations where you choose to save converted files. Selected files are processed on your device; the app does not upload their contents to us. We do not use this access to browse or collect other files on your device. Your files remain under your control.

3. Internet Connection and Third-Party Data

World Macro and Currency Converter connect to external data services to retrieve the economic indicators or exchange rates you request. These requests do not include your name or account because the apps do not have user accounts. Like other internet connections, a request may expose basic technical connection information, such as an IP address, to the relevant data provider. We do not use that information to identify you or create a profile. Third-party providers handle connection information under their own privacy policies.

4. Data Storage and Security

Files selected in EPUB2TXT are processed and saved on your device or to a location you choose. We do not receive or maintain copies of those files. We take a data-minimizing approach and do not operate an account database for these apps. Please use your device’s security and backup controls to protect files stored on it.

5. This Website and External Links

This website has no account registration, contact form, advertising, or analytics features. The website’s hosting provider may process basic technical request information, such as IP address, browser details, and request time, as part of delivering and securing the site. We do not use this information to identify or profile visitors. When you follow a link to the App Store, the World Bank, FRED, or another external site, that service’s own privacy policy and practices apply.

6. Children’s Privacy

Our apps do not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy if our apps, website, or data practices change. If we make a material change, we will publish the revised policy here and update its effective date. Please review this page periodically.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions or suggestions about this policy, contact us at hxx_shdt@126.com. You can also visit the relevant App Store product page for app-specific support.
