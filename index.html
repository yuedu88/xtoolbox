EPUB2TXT

Your books, in the format you need.

Convert EPUB ebooks to plain TXT, or turn TXT files into EPUB for your e-reader. Formatting is removed when converting to TXT, and conversion happens locally on your device with no file upload.

  • EPUB to TXT and TXT to EPUB
  • One-tap file conversion
  • Works offline on your device

World Macro

Global GDP & macroeconomic data.

Explore nominal, real, and per-capita GDP rankings, GDP growth, and CPI inflation across more than 200 countries and regions. U.S. indicators include quarterly GDP, CPI, M2 money supply, and Treasury yields. Browse charts and searchable tables using data from the World Bank and Federal Reserve Economic Data (FRED).

  • Global GDP rankings and country comparisons
  • GDP growth and CPI inflation charts
  • U.S. GDP, CPI, M2, and Treasury data
